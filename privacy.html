Re.Claim Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains what information Re.Claim stores, how it is used, and how you can control it. Your recovery information stays in your browser unless you sign in; when you sign in, app data syncs to your account so you can restore it across devices.

Before anything else: Re.Claim is a support tool, not a medical device or a substitute for professional care. In the U.S. or Canada, call or text 988. In the U.S., text HOME to 741741 for the Crisis Text Line. Elsewhere, find local support at Find A Helpline. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

1. Data stored on your device

When you use Re.Claim without signing in, your entries stay in this browser's local storage. This includes journal entries, mood and craving logs, your sobriety start date, habits, goals, pledges, coping cards, reminders, safety-plan data, and app settings. Clearing browser data or changing devices can remove this local copy, so export a backup if you need one.

2. Account sync

When you sign in, Re.Claim automatically syncs your app data to Google Firebase Cloud Firestore and checks for newer data when the app opens or returns to the foreground. The synced snapshot can include journal text, mood and craving logs, recovery goals, safety-plan details, partner details, and settings. If you enable Encryption in Profile, the app encrypts the full account snapshot on your device before upload; keep your passphrase safe because it is needed to unlock that copy. Without that option, the snapshot is stored in your Firebase account and protected by Firebase access rules and Google's encryption at rest. Firebase connections use TLS while data is in transit. See [Firebase's Firestore encryption overview](https://firebase.google.com/docs/firestore/enterprise/server-side-encryption).

3. Partner features

When you create an invite code, the code, your account email, display name, language, and expiry are stored in Firestore so another signed-in person can connect. A code is intended to be shared directly with someone you trust and expires after 24 hours. Once connected, messages are stored in Firestore and delivered to the two account addresses in that conversation. Messages are encrypted in transit and at rest by Firebase, but they are not end-to-end encrypted. Partner progress is a separate, explicit share containing your sober-day count and activity totals; it is not sent until you tap Share My Progress. Journal text and private notes are not included in that progress share. Local partner notes and check-ins are part of your account snapshot when signed in.

4. Push notifications

If you enable push notifications, your device token and platform are stored in Firebase so notifications you request can reach your device. Re.Claim's notification service reads your saved notification preferences and reminder schedule to send those notifications. You can turn notifications off in your device settings or in the app.

5. Research setting

The current “Share anonymous data” setting only creates a summary on your device. It does not upload or send that summary. The research collection service is not active. Journal text, names, email addresses, and contact details are not included in that local summary.

6. What we do not collect or do

  • No advertising, ad trackers, or analytics SDKs are included.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information.
  • We do not sell advertisements based on your data.
  • We do not store plaintext passwords. Account passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication, and device lock credentials are stored only as salted hashes.

7. Security

Optional app encryption encrypts your account snapshot on your device before cloud upload. The separate Privacy Lock is an access screen for this device; it is not a replacement for account encryption. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted. Use a unique account password, keep your device locked, and use a passphrase you can remember if you enable app encryption. Firebase describes its Firestore encryption in transit and at rest in its encryption overview.

8. Children

Re.Claim is intended for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has been using the app, use the data deletion tools in Profile or the erasure option for local data.

9. Your control and deletion

  • Export: Profile → Export My Data downloads your full data as a JSON file and your journal as text.
  • Erase local data: Profile → Erase my data permanently removes everything stored on the current device.
  • Delete account: Profile → Delete Account removes your Firebase account and the account data collections managed by Re.Claim, then clears the local copy. Messages sent to another person may remain in that person's records.

10. Third-party services

Re.Claim uses Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Messaging) for accounts, sync, partner features, and push notifications. Optional QR-code previews are requested from an image service. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Each service is governed by its own privacy policy.

Remember: your progress is your own. Re.Claim works on your side, in your pocket. If your scores on a self-check feel like a lot to carry, please talk to a trusted person or a professional — you deserve help, and you are not alone.
